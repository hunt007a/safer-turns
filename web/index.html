          How Safer Turns works

          It finds a route that turns left at traffic signals instead of across oncoming traffic where there is no signal, then hands it to Google Maps with up to 3 stops that keep Google on those turns.

          In Toronto, from 2006 to 2023, about 1 in 10 people killed or seriously injured in a crash were in a left-turning vehicle. About 35% of those were at intersections with no traffic signal. The data cannot say how much safer each signalized turn is, so the app only shows which turns you skip and what they cost in time.

          If the safer route adds more than your limit (7 minutes or 30% by default), you get the fastest route and a list of the turns to watch.

          Privacy. Your route is worked out on your phone. Nothing about your trip is sent to us. The address search service sees what you type, and Google sees the trip when you open it.

          Signal locations come from OpenStreetMap and can be missing or out of date. You are always the one driving: check the route, and plan before you set off.
